About Us @66 Keerom Street

Where Aviation
Meets Coffee

Welcome to The 777 Coffee Shop, where a love for aviation takes flight in every cup. Our name was inspired by the owner's passion for the iconic Boeing 777 — a symbol of travel, adventure, and connection.

Using carefully sourced single-origin coffee beans, each brew is made to highlight the unique flavors and stories behind every origin. From rich, bold espressos to smooth, aromatic pours, our coffee is designed to delight enthusiasts and casual drinkers alike.

More than just a coffee shop, The 777 is a destination — a space where community, creativity, and a spirit of exploration come together.
