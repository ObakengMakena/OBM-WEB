Since 2019

One fire, one long table, no rush

Fig & Flame started as a supper club in a friend's courtyard. The idea never changed: cook simply over live fire, pour honest wine, and give people a reason to stay an extra hour.

Everything comes off one wood-fired oven. Produce is from growers we know by name. If it's not in season, it's not on the menu.
