Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This policy explains how OBM Solutions handles your personal information, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

OBM Solutions ("we", "us") is a website design business based in Cape Town, South Africa. For any privacy question, contact us at obm.websolutions@gmail.com or +27 79 310 8775.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. When you complete the contact form or message us, that is typically:

  • Your name and, if you share it, your business name
  • Your phone number, WhatsApp number or email address
  • What you need built, your budget range, and anything else you type into the message

We do not collect special categories of personal information, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Why we use it

We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and — if you go ahead — deliver the work. We do not sell your information, and we do not send marketing you did not ask for.

How it is stored and who can see it

Your form message is delivered to our email inbox through Formspree, a form-processing service. It is stored in our email and, where relevant, in the tools we use to run the business. Access is limited to OBM Solutions. These providers process your information on our behalf and are expected to keep it secure.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to help you, and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records. If you ask us to delete your information, we will — unless we are legally required to keep it.

Your rights under POPIA

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to ask us to delete it. You may also object to how we use it. To exercise any of these, email obm.websolutions@gmail.com and we will respond promptly. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. This does not identify you personally and does not track you across other websites. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and social platforms are governed by those companies' own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
