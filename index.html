Questions

Before you ask

How much does a website cost?

Starter runs R1 400–R2 400, Growth R3 200–R6 000 and Premium R7 200–R12 000. You get a fixed quote in writing before anything starts, so the number never moves.

How long does it take?

Usually about seven days from the point we have your content and your deposit. Bigger sites take longer, and we tell you the timeline upfront.

What happens if I don’t like the free demo?

Nothing. You owe us nothing and there is no obligation. That is the whole point of building it first.

Do I need to provide the content?

Text, photos and your logo help enormously and speed everything up. If you don’t have them, we’ll work through it with you — and we can shoot basic photos on the day.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

We set both up with you. They’re billed by the provider rather than by us, so you keep control and you’re never locked in.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is paid, the site and the domain are yours. You can take them anywhere.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, and we show you how before handover. If you’d rather not, a monthly care plan means we handle it for you.

Do you work outside Cape Town?

Cape Town and the Peninsula for in-person meetings. Everything else we can do remotely, anywhere in South Africa.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We’ll build a demo of the new version first so you can compare it against what you have now.
